# The Nominee Screen Widens From One Island to a Whole Province, and the Revenue Department Has Joined It

<KeyTakeaways>

- **The register, not a sample**: all 25,864 registered juristic persons in Surat Thani, up from an island-by-island approach.[^1][^2]
- **Two new indicators, not two offences**: 5,486 entities share a registered address, 4,621 individuals hold shares across multiple companies; screening criteria, not findings.[^1][^3]
- **Revenue has entered the campaign**: 110 entities examined, 12 concluded involving THB 7.538 million, 98 continuing.[^1][^3]
- **Better-than-Freehold™ takeaway**: no foreign shareholding ratio, no shared address with hundreds of other filings, no pattern for this screen to find.

## What Did the Provincial Register Screen Find?

**Matichon reports Surat Thani's full register, 25,864 juristic persons, run through the screen**, with 11,721 carrying foreign shareholders and 9,281 above 49 per cent foreign shareholding.[^1][^2][^3] Neither figure counts offenders. Foreign majority ownership is lawful outside FBA-reserved businesses, and under BOI or Treaty of Amity; land is the separate constraint, since a company over the limit may not hold it.[^1]

Koh Samui carries 12,906 entities, Koh Pha Ngan 5,009, both inside the total.[^1][^2]

## Why Shared Addresses and Multi-Company Shareholders Are Indicators, Not Verdicts

**The screen adds two indicators: 5,486 entities sharing a registered address, and 4,621 individuals holding shares across multiple companies.**[^1][^3] Neither is wrongdoing alone. A registered office at a law firm or accountancy practice is ordinary business, which is why the count runs so high.

A shared address once sat quietly in a filing cabinet. It now triggers examination automatically, no complaint or raid needed.

## What Carries Forward From Koh Pha Ngan Unchanged

Nothing about the island cohort we reported on 23 August has moved.[^1][^2] The 1,832 land-holding companies on [Koh Pha Ngan already examined](/resources/koh-phangan-112-companies-land-disposal-orders) still produce 112 over the shareholding limit, 104 newly detected plus 8 court-ruled, 124 plots, 86 rai 3 ngan 42.4 square wah. Eight orders to sell nine plots, 28 before the governor, 76 compiled.

New is the frame: Koh Pha Ngan is one data point inside a 25,864-company screen, plus a Revenue enquiry the Land Office cohort never had.

## What the Revenue Department and Labour Checks Add

**The Revenue Department has opened its own examinations, separate from the [Land Office](https://www.dol.go.th) track behind the Koh Pha Ngan orders.** Of 110 entities examined, 12 have concluded involving THB 7.538 million, 98 continuing.[^1][^3] A finding here carries tax consequences regardless of the land outcome, and could feed [AMLO](https://www.amlo.go.th)'s screening where laundering is suspected.

Labour authorities report 7,542 work permits, 4,101 on Koh Samui, 2,513 on Koh Pha Ngan; 104 workers and 46 employers prosecuted.[^1][^2] Named agencies span the [DBD](https://www.dbd.go.th) register filed with the [Ministry of Commerce](https://www.moc.go.th), the Land Office, Revenue, provincial administration, labour and police.[^1]
