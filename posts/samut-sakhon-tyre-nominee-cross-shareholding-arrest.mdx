## How Did Three Companies Holding Shares in Each Other Add Up to Full Control?

**None of the three numbers crossed the line owners watch.** Ketongly, AEC and KTL recorded his stake at 39%, 41% and 40% respectively, each inside the 51/49 split.[^1] Investigators concluded Ketongly and KTL's cross-holdings still amounted to his effective 100% ownership, while at AEC three Thai employees allegedly held the shares, and police say he admitted retaining full control through them.[^1] A land title deed worth THB 9 million was among the documents, the only property in the case.[^1]

He is separately wanted by China over alleged corruption in a government project under a May 2016 warrant; facial recognition identified him, and two more alleged nominee shareholders are being pursued.[^1] The case also touched civil registration: police allege a teenager was given an identity built on a deceased child's birth certificate, supplied by a Thai woman.[^2]

## Is Nominee Conduct Itself Now a Predicate Offence?

**Not yet, and the distinction matters.** AMLO's legal adviser, Peerathorn Vimollohakarn, said corruption-linked assets could be seized because corruption is already a predicate offence under Thailand's Anti-Money Laundering Act.[^3] That applies now. Holding shares through a Thai name for a foreign beneficiary is not yet on that list. A February 2025 Cabinet package would add it, but the December 2025 House dissolution interrupted passage: direction, not deadline.

The finding sits under the [Foreign Business Act](/resources/foreign-business-act-thailand), not the [Anti-Money Laundering Act](https://www.amlo.go.th). The [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th), under the [Ministry of Commerce](https://www.moc.go.th), administers it. Section 36 carries up to three years and/or THB 100,000 to 1,000,000, reaching nominee and beneficiary alike, plus court-ordered cessation of the shareholding and daily fines of THB 10,000 to 50,000, not dissolution. Land held this way can separately face forced disposal by the [Land Department](https://www.dol.go.th).
