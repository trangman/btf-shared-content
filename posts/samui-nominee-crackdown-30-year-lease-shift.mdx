## What Did Officials Say, and What Actually Separates a Lease From a Cover?

**Foreign-controlled companies are moving from buying land into 30-year leases instead**, Polapee Suwunchwee told the conference, a way to dodge liability once the company route grew dangerous.[^1][^2] Daily News called it an evolution of the nominee model, not its replacement: multi-layered companies paired with long-term leasing.[^3]

The instrument changed. The intent did not. A lease registered at the Land Office in the foreigner's own name, within the statutory 30-year maximum, at a rent actually paid and declared, is a right the state can already see in full. What officials described is different in kind: leases layered over companies whose shareholding isn't what it appears; in one case aired, rent moved partly in cash and partly from a foreign bank.[^2] Transparency is the only variable this campaign screens for.

## What Did the Screening Find?

**The Land Department's screen, disclosed at the conference, found 3,892 companies holding land on Samui**: 138 majority foreign-owned, 2,579 with nominee characteristics, against Surat Thani's 25,603 juridical entities overall.[^2] A 90-day verification target applies to the remainder.

The wider register review saw 12,906 Samui companies examined by the [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th), under the [Ministry of Commerce](https://www.moc.go.th): 8,254 carry foreign shareholders and 875 nominee characteristics.[^3] The morning raid found 59 companies worth about THB 1.2 billion; by the closing conference, Daily News put it at 61 companies and about THB 1.5 billion.[^3]

## Why Are Lawyers and Accountants Now Under Notice?

**Samran Nualma put Thai law firms and accounting practices on notice**, warning that the bodies regulating them would be engaged over anyone helping clients structure around the law.[^1][^3] Firms assisting nominee setups would themselves be examined, not only the companies they created.[^3]

It's the first time this campaign has named the advisers, not only the structures, in step with [Financial Action Task Force](https://www.fatf-gafi.org) [gatekeeper standards](/resources/gatekeeper-liability-definition-thailand). An owner whose adviser called a lease over a high-risk company a fix should treat that advice as now inside the enforcement frame; [the adviser's exposure](/resources/lawyer-liability-amla-thailand) doesn't reduce the owner's own.

## What Does This Mean for Existing Owners?

Papering a lease over a high-risk property-owning company doesn't cure the position; it might now draw attention in its own right. With 3,892 land-holding companies under review and a 90-day target attached, the window for a private decision is closing.

Section 37 of the [Foreign Business Act](/resources/foreign-business-act-thailand) reaches the Thai nominee and foreign beneficiary alike: up to three years plus THB 100,000 to 1,000,000, plus forced disposal by the [Land Department](https://www.dol.go.th) and dissolution by the courts. A planned [divestment](/resources/nominee-company-risks-thailand) runs eight to sixteen weeks, expected to reduce to under four weeks as Better-than-Freehold™ grows.
