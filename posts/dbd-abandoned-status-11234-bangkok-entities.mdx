# DBD to Strike Off 11,234 Bangkok Entities: What 'Abandoned' Status Means for Property-Holding Companies

## Thailand's Business Registrar Moves to Strike 11,234 Dormant Bangkok Companies From the Register

<KeyTakeaways>

- **11,234 entities flagged**: 8,446 have not filed for three straight years; 2,788 began dissolution but never finished it.[^2][^3]
- **19 August publication**: the named list goes up on dbd.go.th, giving affected entities 90 days to object.[^3]
- **Liability survives strike-off**: the company loses capacity to transact, but directors, managers, partners and shareholders stay personally liable.[^3][^4]
- **Better-than-Freehold™ takeaway**: no dormant company to keep alive means nothing for a register cleanup to catch.

</KeyTakeaways>

## What Is 'Abandoned' Status Under Thai Company Law?

<QuickAnswer>

**Quick Answer:** "Abandoned" (ร้าง) status strikes a company or partnership from the register, usually after three years without a filed financial statement or an incomplete dissolution. A struck-off entity cannot transact, though its directors, managers and shareholders stay personally liable, and restoring it needs a court application within ten years.[^3][^4]

## What the DBD Announced

Each entity has 90 days from the 19 August publication to lodge a clarification or objection with the [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th) before strike-off proceeds.[^3] This year's total runs roughly 20 per cent above the 9,343 entities covered by the equivalent exercise announced in September 2025.[^5] Objecting does not mean dissolving the company. It means proving the entity is active, which for most non-filers means producing three years of avoided statements.

## What Happens After a Company Is Struck Off

Strike-off removes a company's legal-entity status. It can no longer sign, sell, mortgage or defend title to property it holds. That protection does not reach the people behind it: liabilities of managing partners, directors, managers and shareholders stay fully enforceable, and reversal means a court application within ten years.[^3][^4]

Most coverage underplays this. A struck-off company is not closed. It is a live liability wrapped around a paralysed asset.

## What This Means for At-Risk Property-Owning Companies

Someone still inside a high-risk or at-risk property-owning company should treat this list as a closing window. A company that has not filed for three years is a strike-off candidate and, at the same time, exactly the pattern [nominee screening](/resources/nominee-company-risks-thailand) is built to catch. And a struck-off company cannot execute the transfer documents a divestment requires.

Once capacity to transact is gone, divestment, typically eight to sixteen weeks and expected to shorten to under four as Better-than-Freehold™ grows, becomes a court matter rather than a Land Office one. Nominee enforcement, separately, still pairs forced disposal by the [Land Department](https://www.dol.go.th) with dissolution by the courts; the [Anti-Money Laundering Act](https://www.amlo.go.th) predicate-offence amendment remains unenacted, direction rather than deadline. Acting before 19 August keeps the exit in the owner's hands.

## Impact on Foreign Property Buyers

Anyone weighing a Thai company under the [Ministry of Commerce](https://www.moc.go.th)'s register as the route into a purchase should price in the upkeep. A company carries a filing obligation for every year of ownership, and three missed years is exactly what triggers a cleanup like this one. The route was never compliant for [controlling Thai land](/resources/foreign-business-act-thailand). This just adds a maintenance penalty on top.
