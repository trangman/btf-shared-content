## What Happened in Pattaya on 21 August 2026?

<QuickAnswer>

**Quick Answer:** On 21 August 2026, two Thai ministers led DSI and police searches of three Pattaya addresses under Criminal Court warrants over suspected nominee shareholding under the Foreign Business Act. Officers took documents and electronic devices for examination; nobody has been arrested or charged, and DSI has not publicly named the companies or individuals involved.[^1]

## Why a Law Office Came First

The Pattaya News places the law office first, ahead of the condominium and the hotel.[^1] Officers went to the desk that drew the paperwork up, not to a property. The [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th), under the [Ministry of Commerce](https://www.moc.go.th), keeps that register. [Gatekeeper liability](/resources/gatekeeper-liability-definition-thailand) is now the address on a warrant.

## What the Three Addresses Have in Common

Media reporting, not DSI, supplies these names.[^1][^3] The Pattaya News names the office as KT International Law and Business, the condominium company as Cosy Beach View Company Limited in Nong Prue, and the third as Lotus Paradise Development, a hotel business.[^1]

The Pattaya News and Thai Examiner report that KT International registered Cosy Beach View in 2012, and that the law office's owner appears in Lotus Paradise Development's shareholder records for 2013 to 2015.[^1][^3] Thai Rath separately traces alleged nominee conduct to 2010, a distinct claim, not the registration date.[^2]

The Pattaya News and Thai Examiner identify Cosy Beach View's director only as "Mr M", an Israeli national holding 49% of the shares.[^1][^3] Thai Rath, Thai Examiner and The Pattaya News all call the wider network Israeli-linked.[^1][^2][^3] That 49% stake is lawful on its face. A DBD screening pool announced on 20 August 2026 counts 31,516 of 36,277 foreign-linked landholding entities at holdings of 49% or less; the test is whether the Thai shareholder genuinely funded the stake.

## What Has Not Been Confirmed

Officers seized registration documents, computers and electronic data for forensic examination.[^1] Nothing describes land, units or company assets as seized, frozen or confiscated; one headline's "seized fortune" describes a valuation under investigation, not property taken.[^2] No arrest and no charge has been reported anywhere.[^1][^2][^3][^4] DSI said the case would proceed strictly on the evidence and the law.[^1] The hotel is not named here; two outlets transliterate its name differently.
