# AMLO Seizes 1,080 Assets in a THB 287 Million Round, and Rejects Owners' Objections in Thirteen More Cases

## AMLO's Latest Round Shows How Far Forfeiture Reaches Into Land

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Broad seizure round**: 37 cases, 1,080 assets, about THB 287 million seized or frozen; the meeting's full tally came to 1,486 assets across 54 cases, worth over THB 605 million.[^2]
- **Chen Zhi tranche grows**: a further THB 115 million, including land with structures, pushes his case past THB 460 million.[^1][^2]
- **Thirteen cases move to forfeiture**: 376 assets, about THB 317 million, after weak objections.[^2]

## How the Chen Zhi Case Reached Land and Buildings

Chen Zhi, 38, founded Prince Group, a Cambodian conglomerate spanning financial and real estate businesses in over 30 countries. Sanctioned by the US, he was extradited to China in January 2026, awaiting trial.

The 10 August order adds 431 assets worth about THB 115 million, cash, gold, land with structures, digital assets, under order Y.169/2569.[^1][^2] Added to an earlier THB 345 million order (black case number ฟ 25/2569), the total passes THB 460 million.[^1][^2]

Neither source says how the land was held. This is a money-laundering and scam-network matter, not a nominee case. What it shows is that land with structures is seized on the same footing as cash and gold.

## Why Thirteen Cases Are Now Headed for Forfeiture

Thirteen cases, 376 assets, about THB 317 million: all referred for forfeiture, for the same reason each time.[^2] Objections filed did not carry enough weight to show the property was unconnected to the offences.

The mechanism repays attention. AMLA forfeiture is civil, against the asset, run by the [Anti-Money Laundering Office](https://www.amlo.go.th)'s Transaction Committee, distinct from a criminal case.[^3] It needs no criminal conviction, only that the claimant's evidence fell short. One involved 103 assets, about THB 84 million, from customs smuggling (Y.120/2569).[^2] Four smaller cases went the other way, for victims.[^2]

## What This Means for Owners of High-Risk Property-Owning Companies

None of the predicate offences in this batch, narcotics, fraud, gambling, categories aligned with [FATF](https://www.fatf-gafi.org) standards, describes an ordinary owner. But the mechanism, land reached, burden on the claimant, applies whenever the Transaction Committee looks at any property. Existing law already reaches [nominee conduct](/resources/nominee-company-risks-thailand) hard: up to three years plus a THB 100,000 to 1,000,000 fine under [Foreign Business Act](/resources/foreign-business-act-thailand) section 36, reaching Thai nominee and foreign beneficiary, and enforcement pairs forced disposal by the [Department of Lands](https://www.dol.go.th) with court-ordered cessation of the offending shareholding.

The only answer to a seizure order is documentary proof of a legitimate beneficial interest, often the very paperwork evidencing the constraint being avoided. Divestment, eight to sixteen weeks and expected to fall under four as Better-than-Freehold™ grows, is best done before any order lands. A February 2025 Cabinet package would add nominee conduct as a [predicate offence](/resources/anti-money-laundering-act-thailand); December's dissolution stalled it, direction not deadline. For an alternative rather than an exit, choose a structure recorded on [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th) and Land Department registers, not a side agreement no court must credit.
